## 🧰 Shared Skills

Open-source skills for running a household with an AI agent — buying used, booking flights, keeping
Drive tidy, fixing the printer, designing parts, and a set of advisory teams for the legal, tax and
health paperwork every family carries. Each is a folder in the open
[Agent Skills](https://agentskills.io) format, so it works with **Codex, Gemini CLI, GitHub
Copilot, Antigravity, Claude Code** or any harness that reads `SKILL.md`. Nothing depends on one
vendor's tools. MIT licensed.

**Install:** `tools/install-skills.sh <your-project> [skill ...]` links skills into every harness's
folder at once — see [docs/install.md](docs/install.md). Skills that need your details (home
airport, zip, printers) read `config/household.yaml`; copy the `household.example.yaml` beside it,
or let the skill ask you.

**Browse them all:** the [Agent Explorer](https://don-petry.github.io/don-petry/agents-explorer.html) is a
searchable catalog of every skill, persona and workflow here, generated by `tools/agents_explorer.py`
([source](docs/agents-explorer.html)).

### Household advisory teams

Three teams share one contract — [docs/advisory-teams.md](docs/advisory-teams.md): a capacity gate
and escalation table run before any work, every figure comes from a document or an engine run, and
every citation passes `tools/verify_sources.py` before a draft leaves `drafts/`. They prepare the
hand-off to a licensed professional; none of them is legal, tax, investment or medical advice, and
none files, pays, signs or sends. Jurisdiction is configured per household (Alabama is the worked
example).

| Skill | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [household-onboarding](skills/household-onboarding/SKILL.md) | Builds a sourced picture of the household across 14 areas for the teams: discovery first, interviews only on conflicts, gaps and intent (at most 12 questions), answers written back where the teams read them, coverage tracked. Includes a phone question page. |
| [legal](skills/legal/SKILL.md) | Household legal guide: capacity-and-escalation gate first, matter file, drafts verified against primary sources before anything reaches `outbound/`, and a lawyer hand-off packet when an escalation fires. |
| [tax-wealth](skills/tax-wealth/SKILL.md) | Household tax and wealth team: projections, year-end moves, estimated payments, education funding, retirement and Social Security scenarios — every figure from a stamped PolicyEngine-US or Owl engine run. Prepares the CPA packet. |
| [tax-wealth-returns](skills/tax-wealth-returns/SKILL.md) | Extracts filed federal and state returns from the PDFs into line-by-line JSON with page references and cross-checks, and keeps a multi-year table. |
| [tax-wealth-job-offer](skills/tax-wealth-job-offer/SKILL.md) | Weighs a job offer on the tax and wealth side: cash vs. equity, ISO/NSO, 83(b), QSBS, AMT, break-even exits, and the benefits lost by leaving. |
| [tax-wealth-intake](skills/tax-wealth-intake/SKILL.md) | Builds the household fact finder the teams rely on — people, authority documents, dependents, accounts — with a source on every fact and only real gaps listed as questions. |
| [health-advocate](skills/health-advocate/SKILL.md) | Household health team lead: who-may-act gate (minors, adult children, HIPAA authorizations), appeals by plan track, Medicaid coordination, year-end benefit use and open enrollment. |
| [eob-sync](skills/eob-sync/SKILL.md) | Insurer-agnostic EOB store and checker: denials, balance billing, provider bills above the EOB, appeal clocks, missed premiums, expected claims, deductible and out-of-pocket position. Read-only. |
| [hsa-fsa-reimburse](skills/hsa-fsa-reimburse/SKILL.md) | HSA and limited-purpose FSA shoebox: holds claims with unsettled eligibility, routes dental to the FSA, matches card payments, builds packets, records what was filed. Never submits. |

**Personas** ([agents/](agents/)) — specialists a skill can call, or you can read inline:
[red-team](agents/red-team.md) argues the other side of a draft (adjuster, examiner, opposing
party); [source-verifier](agents/source-verifier.md) traces every citation, figure and date to a
primary source; [legal-intake](agents/legal-intake.md),
[tax-wealth-intake](agents/tax-wealth-intake.md) and [health-intake](agents/health-intake.md) build each
team's matter or member file; [tax-wealth-equity-analyst](agents/tax-wealth-equity-analyst.md) maps
equity-compensation tax events; [health-benefits-analyst](agents/health-benefits-analyst.md) handles
HSA/FSA and enrollment money questions; [onboarding-discovery](agents/onboarding-discovery.md) and
[onboarding-interviewer](agents/onboarding-interviewer.md) run household onboarding.

`verify_sources.py` (fail-closed citation checker with pluggable jurisdiction adapters),
`agents_explorer.py` (builds the catalog page), `mojibake_guard.py` (stops agents writing `â€"`/`?`
